Disclosures

The Home Price Estimator is provided for entertainment and informational purposes only. It is not financial advice, a loan approval, a pre-qualification, a pre-approval, or an offer to lend.

Purpose of the Home Price Estimator

The Home Price Estimator is designed to provide an illustrative starting point for exploring home affordability based on the information and assumptions entered by the user.

The estimator uses mathematical calculations, estimated costs, averages, and other assumptions to produce an illustrative home price example.

The results are intended to help users explore what a potential home purchase might look like based on a monthly housing payment they feel comfortable with and the funds they have available for a down payment and closing costs.

Not a Loan Qualification

The Home Price Estimator does not determine whether a person qualifies for a mortgage.

Use of the estimator does not constitute a mortgage application, pre-qualification, pre-approval, loan approval, commitment to lend, or offer to lend.

The payment amount a user feels comfortable with and the payment amount for which the user may qualify are not necessarily the same.

Mortgage qualification may depend on income, employment, debts, assets, credit history, loan program requirements, property characteristics, underwriting guidelines, and other factors.

Illustrative Results

Results produced by the Home Price Estimator are illustrative examples only.

Actual mortgage payments, interest rates, property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, closing costs, prepaid expenses, and other costs may differ from the estimates produced by the tool.

No guarantee is made that the results will accurately predict the terms, costs, payment, or eligibility associated with an actual mortgage transaction.

Interest Rate

The Home Price Estimator uses an example mortgage interest rate based on a published market survey and applicable estimator assumptions.

The example rate is not a rate offered or guaranteed to any individual borrower.

Actual interest rates may vary based on market conditions, borrower qualifications, loan characteristics, property characteristics, and lender requirements.

Property Taxes

Property tax estimates are based on published historical data and effective property tax rates. These rates represent broad averages and may not reflect the actual tax amount for a particular property.

Actual property taxes may vary by property, municipality, county, township, school district, assessment, exemptions, purchase price, reassessment, and other factors.

Homeowners Insurance

Homeowners insurance estimates are based on published averages and estimator assumptions. Actual insurance premiums vary by property, location, coverage, deductible, insurer, claims history, and other factors.

The Home Price Estimator does not obtain an insurance quote for a specific property.

Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI)

When applicable, the Home Price Estimator uses illustrative assumptions to estimate monthly private mortgage insurance.

Actual PMI pricing is determined by mortgage insurance providers and may depend on borrower and loan characteristics that are not evaluated by the estimator.

The PMI amount shown by the estimator should therefore be considered an illustrative estimate and not a quote or commitment from a mortgage insurance provider.

Closing Costs

Closing costs shown by the Home Price Estimator are estimates based on typical ranges and assumptions.

Actual closing costs may include lender fees, title services, homeowners insurance, property taxes, government recording fees, prepaid expenses, escrow deposits, prorated items, and other transaction-specific costs.

Actual costs vary by property, location, lender, transaction structure, and borrower circumstances.

Association Fees

The Home Price Estimator does not include association fees in its estimated monthly housing payment.

If a property has an association fee, such as a condominium, townhome, or planned unit development fee, the user should reduce the monthly payment amount entered into the estimator by the anticipated association fee.

Failure to account for an association fee could result in an estimated home price that is higher than the user may actually find comfortable.

Flood Insurance

For simplicity, the Home Price Estimator assumes no flood insurance expense.

If a property is located within a designated Special Flood Hazard Area (SFHA), flood insurance may be required by the lender and could increase the total monthly housing payment.

Because flood insurance requirements vary by property and location, the estimator does not attempt to estimate this cost.

Property Type and Financing Assumptions

Unless otherwise indicated, the Home Price Estimator assumes an owner-occupied, single-family, primary residence using conventional conforming, fixed-rate financing with at least a 5% down payment.

Other mortgage programs and property types may have different requirements, costs, terms, down payment options, loan limits, or eligibility requirements and are outside the scope of this tool.

Data Sources and Methodology

The Home Price Estimator uses published information, averages, assumptions, and mathematical calculations to produce its illustrative results.

Data sources and methodologies may include published mortgage market surveys, property tax information, homeowners insurance averages, mortgage insurance assumptions, and typical closing cost ranges.

All assumptions, methodologies, and data sources are subject to change without notice.

No Guarantee

Although the Home Price Estimator is designed to use reasonable assumptions and accurate mathematical calculations, no guarantee is made regarding the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, or applicability of any result.

Users should independently verify information that is important to their home purchase decision and consult appropriately licensed professionals for advice specific to their circumstances.
