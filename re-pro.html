Home Price Estimator for Real Estate Professionals

The OwnerPal Home Price Estimator is simply a calculator.

The image below can be added to your website to open the calculator in a new browser tab, while never leaving your website.

When a user closes the calculator, they are back on your website, where you control the narrative.

A Conversation Starter

Instead of waiting until a buyer has fallen in love with a property online, OwnerPal Home Price Estimator can help start the affordability conversation earlier.

That can mean fewer surprises, less wasted time, and potentially less emotional disappointment when a particular price point does not fit the buyer's overall budget.

Put Your Local Expertise into the Conversation

You know the local market. Property taxes, homeowners’ insurance, interest rates, and other housing costs can vary considerably from one area to another.

OwnerPal’s Interactive What-If Calculator lets users adjust key assumptions and see how those changes can affect potential home price affordability. This gives you an opportunity to bring your local knowledge into the conversation rather than leaving buyers to interpret every number on their own.

Use the results to explore questions such as: What is different in this market? Which costs should be adjusted? What happens if rates, taxes, or insurance are higher or lower?
